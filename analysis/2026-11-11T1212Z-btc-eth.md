## What would change this view

- Repeated synchronized observations showing ETH no longer underperforming BTC would change the relative-weakness description; a single quote would not establish persistence.
- Falling coin-denominated open interest alongside subsequent lower prices would replace the present description of expanding outstanding exposure with one of contraction. Open interest would still not identify the liquidating side.
- Cross-venue funding becoming uniformly negative, or Binance cohort ratios losing their long lean, would change the measured positioning description without establishing motives.
- Fresh, independently reconciled ETF flows for a later US trading day and an updated weekly CFTC report would replace the lagged flow and futures-category context.
- Persistent changes in option put/call distribution, together with new DVOL observations, would change the expiry-specific exposure description; neither would turn max pain into a forecast.
- Reconciled stablecoin chart totals with source observation timestamps, restored MVRV records, and current network measurements would allow a better-supported on-chain assessment.
- New Treasury observations and fact-recorded release outcomes would update the macro context; attributing price moves to a release would still require evidence beyond temporal coincidence.
